## Getting Started

### Accessing the Platform
Open your web browser and navigate to the platform URL.

### Supported Languages
Click the **globe icon** (🌐) in the top navigation bar to switch between:
English, 中文, বাংলা, हिन्दी, العربية, Nederlands, Français

### Supported Browsers
Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest 2 versions)

---

## For Students

### 1. Create an Account
1. Click **Register** in the top-right corner
2. Select **Student**
3. Fill in your name, email, and password
4. Click **Register**

### 2. Set Up Your Profile
1. After login, go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **Profile** in the navigation
3. Fill in your major, grade, year, skills, and availability
4. Click **Save**

### 3. Create a Resume
1. From your Dashboard, click **Create Resume**
2. Fill in your education details, skills, work experience
3. Click **Save**
4. You can create multiple resumes and set one as default

### 4. Find and Apply to Jobs
1. Click **Jobs** in the navigation
2. Use the search bar and location filter
3. Click a job card to see details
4. Select your resume from the dropdown
5. Click **Apply Now**

### 5. Track Your Applications
1. Click **Applications** in the navigation
2. View the status of each application:
   - **Pending**: Submitted, awaiting review
   - **Reviewing**: Employer is looking at your application
   - **Interview**: You've been invited for an interview
   - **Hired**: Congratulations!
   - **Rejected**: Not selected for this position

### 6. Save Jobs
- Click **Save** on any job to bookmark it for later
- View saved jobs from your Dashboard

## For Enterprises (Employers)

### 1. Register Your Company
1. Click **Register** → Select **Employer**
2. Fill in your email and password
3. After login, complete your company profile:
   - Company name
   - Contact person and phone
   - Address and website
   - Company description
4. Wait for admin approval

### 2. Post a Job
1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **Post a Job**
3. Fill in:
   - Job title
   - Salary and working hours
   - Location and duration
   - Required skills (comma-separated)
   - Responsibilities
4. Click **Submit**
5. The job will be reviewed by an admin before going live

### 3. Manage Your Jobs
1. Click **Manage Jobs**
2. You can:
   - **Pause/Activate** a job listing
   - **Edit** job details
   - **Delete** a job
3. View views and application counts

### 4. Review Applicants
1. Click **Applicants** in the navigation
2. For each applicant, you can:
   - Mark as **Reviewing**
   - **Schedule an Interview** (set date, location, notes)
   - **Hire** or **Reject**
3. View the applicant's resume

## For Administrators

### 1. Dashboard Overview
The admin dashboard shows:
- Total users, students, enterprises
- Pending jobs awaiting review
- Quick action links

### 2. Verify Students
1. Go to **Verify Students**
2. Review student details
3. Click **Verify** to approve

### 3. Approve Enterprises
1. Go to **Approve Enterprises**
2. Review company details
3. Click **Approve** to activate

### 4. Audit Jobs
1. Go to **Audit Jobs**
2. Review pending job postings
3. Click **Approve** to make the job live
4. Or **Reject** with a reason

### 5. Manage Users
1. Go to **User Management**
2. View all platform users
3. **Ban** or **Unban** users as needed

### 6. Manage Content
- **Banners**: Add/edit/remove homepage banners
- **Announcements**: Create announcements or articles for the platform

## Password Reset
1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your inbox for a reset link
4. Follow the link to set a new password

## Notifications
- The **bell icon** (🔔) shows unread notifications
- Click it to see recent notifications
- Click **View all** for the full list
- Notifications appear for:
  - Application status changes
  - Interview schedules
  - Verification and approval updates

## FAQ

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.

**Q: Why can't I see my job listing?**
A: New jobs require admin approval. Check the status in Manage Jobs.

**Q: Can I edit my application after submitting?**
A: No, but you can submit a new application with a different resume.

**Q: How do I change the language?**
A: Click the globe icon in the top navigation bar.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. All data is stored in Firebase with security rules and encrypted in transit via HTTPS.
